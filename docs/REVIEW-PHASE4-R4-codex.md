7. [NEW-ERROR] Scheduler health still fails open and can lose the failure it is meant to surface. The daemon ignores `atomicwrite.Write` errors, so a failed clear leaves a stale OFF forever and a failed error write leaves doctor reporting healthy (`cmd/nexus/main.go:133-148`). In addition, health is sampled only every 30 seconds by a ticker started beside the scheduler's own 30-second ticker; a successful next sweep may replace the startup error before it is ever mirrored. The mirror-failure RED proves only `Sweep`/in-memory `Health`, not daemon persistence or doctor behavior (`internal/schedule/schedule_test.go:563-584`). Mirror health on each sweep result (or consume an error transition), surface mirror-write failure, and make doctor distinguish absent, empty, non-empty, and unreadable states.
